Damage Prevention

Commercial Water Damage Risk Assessment and Prevention

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions on their properties. Injuries, lawsuits, and damaged property can all trace back to a sudden failure from inadequate waterproofing, concealed moisture, or plumbing past its prime. Riverview Restoration provides professional water damage risk assessments using moisture monitoring equipment and thermal imaging technology that evaluate every area's vulnerability to water intrusion. You receive a written report that lists each risk, ranks it by priority, and spells out the preventive steps to take.

These assessments serve a dual purpose: they protect people and property by identifying vulnerabilities, and they demonstrate due diligence that can protect you legally if an incident occurs. Owners who keep up regular professional inspections and follow the experts' advice are viewed favorably by insurers and courts. Our recommendation is a moisture assessment once a year for every commercial property, and more frequent checks in high-risk spots like mechanical rooms, basements, and older plumbing systems.
