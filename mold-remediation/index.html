Beyond looking bad, mold endangers health and tells you there is a moisture problem underneath, one that can weaken the building itself. Mold exposure can cause respiratory issues, allergic reactions, headaches and more severe health effects, particularly in children, the elderly and people with compromised immune systems. At Riverview Restoration, our IICRC-certified mold remediation technicians follow strict industry protocols to safely contain, remove and prevent mold in Riverview homes and commercial properties.

Effective mold remediation goes far beyond scrubbing visible mold off surfaces. A professional job has five parts: locate the water source that feeds the growth, build proper containment against cross-contamination, use HEPA-filtered tools to remove damaged materials safely, treat surfaces left behind with an antimicrobial solution, and resolve the moisture problem at its root to stop recurrence. The result of that thorough process is mold dealt with at its source, never simply masked.

We begin every mold remediation project with a thorough inspection and assessment, often including air quality testing to determine the type and concentration of mold spores present. With that data we can build a remediation plan aimed at exactly what your property needs. Throughout the project we maintain negative air pressure containment, run HEPA air scrubbers and follow the IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation. Afterward we verify the results, with clearance air testing if you request it, to confirm the property is safe to occupy.

The Benefits of Professional Mold Remediation

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. Clean off the visible growth but leave the moisture problem, and the mold comes back, in many cases in a matter of weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.

Signs You Need Mold Remediation in Riverview

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Furniture against outside walls or items in closets developing speckled patches
  • Stains spreading on a ceiling or wall, or dark marks ringing the air vents
  • Water damage not long ago that was left to dry without professional equipment
  • Condensation forming regularly on windows, walls or cold surfaces
  • Recurring respiratory symptoms, allergies or headaches that improve when you are away from home
  • Spots you wipe away that keep reappearing in the same place

Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • When windows, piping or other cold surfaces start to sweat, correct it without waiting
  • Clear gutters and downspouts regularly, which keeps rain from soaking the foundation walls
  • Mold-resistant paint and wallboard are the better choice wherever moisture is common, such as a basement or bath
  • Give the crawl space proper ventilation, and think about adding a vapor barrier over the soil
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • Mold in ductwork is prevented by servicing the heating and cooling system and replacing its filters on schedule
